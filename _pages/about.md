I am a **first-year Ph.D. candidate** in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, supervised by **Professor Junxian He**, who also advised me during my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). I graduated from SJTU in June 2024.

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**. My research interests include:

1. **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
2. **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
3. **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

My [publications]({{ base_path }}/publications/) are listed below and on the Publications page.
